# Daily English – 2026-11-18

## Phrasal verbs

### 1. Drop off
**Meaning:** (a) to take someone/something to a place and leave them there (usually by car); (b) to fall asleep without meaning to.
**Context:** driving, deliveries, parcels, kids at school; being tired.
- "Can you drop me off at Central Station on your way to work?" (a)
- "I dropped off the parcel at the post office this morning." (a)
- "I was so tired I dropped off on the couch watching TV." (b)

### 2. Look after
**Meaning:** to take care of someone or something.
**Context:** kids, pets, plants, a house, yourself, customers at work.
- "Could you look after my dog while I'm in Melbourne?"
- "She works as an au pair, looking after two kids."
- "Look after yourself, mate – see you next week!" (very common goodbye in Australia)

### 3. Work out
**Meaning:** (a) to exercise, usually at the gym; (b) to find the answer/solution (similar to "figure out"); (c) to end well/successfully.
**Context:** fitness, problems & maths, plans and situations.
- "I work out at the gym three times a week." (a)
- "I need to work out how much I owe for the bills." (b)
- "Don't worry, everything will work out in the end." (c)

## Verbs

| Verb | Type | Present (he/she) | Past simple | Past participle | -ing |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|------|
| send | irregular | sends | sent | sent | sending |
| feel | irregular | feels | felt | felt | feeling |
| wait | regular | waits | waited | waited | waiting |
| move | regular | moves | moved | moved | moving |

- **send** – to make something go to someone, e.g. a message, email, parcel or money.
  "I sent my resume to five cafés yesterday." / "Have you sent the rent money to the landlord?"
- **feel** – to experience an emotion or a physical sensation.
  "I felt really nervous before my job interview." / "I'm not feeling well, I think I'll stay home today."
- **wait (for)** – to stay where you are or delay doing something until someone/something arrives.
  "I waited for the bus for 30 minutes this morning." / "I'm waiting for my visa to be approved."
- **move** – (a) to change the place where you live or work; (b) to change position.
  "We moved to a new flat in Fitzroy last month." / "Can you move your car? It's blocking the driveway."
