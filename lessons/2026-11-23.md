# Daily English – 2026-11-23

## Phrasal verb: Call back
**Meaning:** to phone someone again, or to return a phone call you missed.
**Context:** missed calls, job applications, doctors, real estate agents, friends.
- "Sorry, I'm on the bus. Can I call you back in 10 minutes?"
- "The agent said she'll call me back about the flat."
- "I missed your call – I'll call you back tonight."

💡 Tip: the person goes in the middle → "call **you** back", "call **him** back".

## Verbs

| Verb | Type | Present (he/she) | Past simple | Past participle | -ing |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|------|
| wake (up) | irregular | wakes | **woke** | **woken** | waking |
| remember | regular | remembers | remembered | remembered | remembering |

### wake (up) – to stop sleeping.
- Present: "I wake up at 6 every day for work."
- Past: "I woke up late and missed the train."
- Present perfect: "Have you woken up yet? It's 10 o'clock!"
- Continuous: "The baby is waking up."

### remember – to keep something in your mind / not forget it.
- Present: "I remember your name – you're Sam, right?"
- Past: "I remembered to bring my keys this time!"
- Present perfect: "Have you remembered to pay the rent?"
- Continuous: "I'm remembering more words every day."

🧠 Easy memory: "I **woke** up and **remembered** to **call** my mum **back**."
