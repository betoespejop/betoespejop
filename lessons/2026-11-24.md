# Daily English – 2026-11-24

## Phrasal verb: Turn down

**Meaning 1:** to say "no" to an offer, invitation or request (reject politely).
**Context:** jobs, invitations, offers, shifts at work.
- "They offered me an extra shift on Sunday, but I turned it down."
- "She turned down the job because the pay was too low."
- "I don't want to turn down your invitation, but I'm busy tonight."

**Meaning 2:** to make something quieter, lower or less strong (volume, heat, air-con).
**Context:** home, share house, car, cooking.
- "Can you turn down the music? My housemate is sleeping."
- "Turn down the heat or the rice will burn."

**Opposite:** *turn up* (louder / accept less common) – "Turn up the volume!"
**Tip to remember:** thumbs DOWN = "no" 👎 and volume DOWN 🔉.

## Verbs

| Verb | Type | Present (I / he) | Past simple | Past participle | -ing |
|------|------|------------------|-------------|-----------------|------|
| choose | irregular | choose / chooses | **chose** | **chosen** | choosing |
| use | regular | use / uses | **used** | **used** | using |

### choose (irregular) – to pick one thing from several options.
- Present: "I always **choose** the cheapest coffee."
- Past: "I **chose** a room close to the train station."
- Present perfect: "Have you **chosen** your course yet?"
- Continuous: "I'm **choosing** a phone plan right now."
- Future: "I **will choose** the beach for the weekend."

**Tip:** choose → chose → chosen (like *speak → spoke → spoken*).

### use (regular) – to do something with an object, tool or service for a purpose.
- Present: "I **use** my Opal card every day."
- Past: "I **used** Google Maps to find the bus stop."
- Present perfect: "Have you **used** the self-checkout at Coles?"
- Continuous: "Sorry, I'm **using** the washing machine."
- Future: "I **will use** my savings to travel to Melbourne."

**Bonus:** *used to* = something you did in the past but not now – "I **used to** live in Colombia."

## Mini story (everything together)
"My boss offered me a night shift, but I **turned it down**. I **chose** to study instead and **used** the free time to practise my English. Then I **turned down** the music and went to sleep."
