# Daily English – 2026-11-13

## Phrasal verbs

### 1. Pick up
**Meaning:** (a) to collect someone/something; (b) to learn something informally, without studying; (c) to lift something.
**Context:** transport, shopping, learning languages, daily chores.
- "Can you pick me up from the station at six?" (a)
- "I'll pick up some bread on the way home." (a)
- "I've picked up a lot of Aussie slang at work." (b)

### 2. Sort out
**Meaning:** to fix, organise or solve a problem or situation.
**Context:** problems, paperwork, bills, arrangements (very common in Australian/British English).
- "I need to sort out my tax file number this week."
- "Don't worry, the landlord will sort it out."
- "We sorted out the plans for Saturday – we're going to Manly."

### 3. Turn up
**Meaning:** (a) to arrive or appear, often unexpectedly or late; (b) to increase the volume/temperature.
**Context:** meetings, parties, work shifts, music, heating.
- "He turned up 20 minutes late to his shift." (a)
- "Only five people turned up to the meeting." (a)
- "Can you turn up the music? I love this song!" (b)

## Verbs

| Verb | Type | Past simple | Past participle | -ing |
|------|------|-------------|-----------------|------|
| spend | irregular | spent | spent | spending |
| find | irregular | found | found | finding |
| book | regular | booked | booked | booking |
| arrive | regular | arrived | arrived | arriving |

- **spend** – to use money to buy things, or to use time doing something.
  "I spent $80 on groceries this week." / "We spent the whole day at the beach."
- **find** – to discover or get something you were looking for (or by chance).
  "I finally found a cheap room near the city." / "Have you found a job yet?"
- **book** – to reserve a place, ticket, table or appointment in advance.
  "I booked a table for four at 7 pm." / "She has booked a doctor's appointment for Monday."
- **arrive** – to reach a place at the end of a trip. (Use *arrive at* a building/place, *arrive in* a city/country.)
  "We arrived in Melbourne late last night." / "What time does the bus arrive at the airport?"
