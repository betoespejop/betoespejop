# Daily English – 2026-11-21

## Phrasal verb: Pick up

**Meaning (3 common uses):**
1. To collect someone or something from a place.
2. To lift something from the floor/table with your hand.
3. To learn something naturally, without studying formally (e.g. a language, an accent).

**Contexts:** driving/transport, shopping, phone calls, learning English in Australia.

- "Can you **pick me up** from the station at 6?" (collect someone)
- "I need to **pick up** my parcel from the post office." (collect something)
- "Please **pick up** your clothes from the floor." (lift)
- "I've **picked up** a lot of Aussie slang since I arrived." (learn naturally)
- Bonus: "**Pick up** the phone!" = answer the phone.

**Easy tip:** *pick* = choose/take + *up* = up from somewhere → you *take* something/someone *from* a place.

## Verbs

| Verb | Type | Present (I/he) | Past simple | Past participle | -ing |
|------|------|----------------|-------------|-----------------|------|
| buy | irregular | buy / buys | **bought** | **bought** | buying |
| book | regular | book / books | **booked** | **booked** | booking |

### buy (irregular) – to get something by paying money for it
- Present: "I **buy** groceries every Sunday."
- Past: "I **bought** a new SIM card yesterday."
- Present perfect: "Have you **bought** your train ticket?"
- Continuous: "I'm **buying** coffee – do you want one?"
- Future: "I **will buy** a bike next month."

**Memory tip:** buy → **bought** sounds like "bot" — "The bot **bought** a bike."

### book (regular) – to reserve something in advance (a table, a ticket, an appointment)
- Present: "I always **book** my flights online."
- Past: "She **booked** a table for four at 7pm."
- Present perfect: "I've **booked** a doctor's appointment for Monday."
- Continuous: "We're **booking** a hostel in Melbourne."
- Future: "I'll **book** the tour tonight."

**Memory tip:** regular = just add **-ed** → book**ed** (pronounced /bʊkt/).

## Mini story (everything together)
"Yesterday I **booked** a car, **picked up** my friend at the airport and we **bought** some snacks for the trip."
